## Evidence behind Scene 3

Every leg of the problem statement was checked against published research
before it was written into the film.

| Claim in the film | Supporting data |
|---|---|
| Tools that never scale | 95% of GenAI pilots deliver no measurable P&L impact (MIT, *The GenAI Divide*); 59% of AI initiatives never reach production (Gartner 2026) |
| Citizen developers everywhere | 41% of employees are citizen developers (Gartner); 65–73% of low-code orgs have no governance rules (KPMG, 715 orgs) |
| Vendor AI ≠ advantage | Feature overlap between competing products rose from ~40% to 85%+ in two years; 80% of buyers call AI commoditization the top risk to SaaS valuations |
| Can't pick high-value opportunities | 71% of CIOs struggle to prioritize use cases with measurable outcomes (Gartner 2026) |
| CFO sees cost, not return | EY 2026: "escalating token costs trigger fiscal scrutiny"; only 7% report established AI ROI (KPMG, 2,145 leaders) |
| Exit-value thesis | Proprietary tech carries a 15–20% valuation premium; correctly deployed AI drives 0.5x–1.5x multiple lift at exit — proprietary IP owned at exit is the lever carrying the durable premium |

## Production

| | |
|---|---|
| Framework | Remotion 4.0.512 (React → real MP4) |
| Format | 1920×1080, 30fps, H.264 |
| Typeface | Poppins, self-hosted (`public/fonts`) — closest available geometric match to the deck's Century Gothic |
| Logo | Extracted from the deck cover, background flood-filled to transparency |
| Runtime | ~4:18 at estimated VO pacing; re-timed exactly once the real MP3 lands |

**Re-timing to the voiceover:**

```bash
cp <your-elevenlabs-file>.mp3 public/voiceover.mp3
node scripts/sync-audio.mjs public/voiceover.mp3
npx remotion render FilmWithAudio out/infinite-possibilities.mp4
```

`sync-audio.mjs` finds the eleven 1.5s breaks in the MP3, derives the twelve
scene durations from them, and rewrites `src/timing.ts`. Every animation delay
is expressed relative to its scene, so nothing else needs touching.
